# Work pack: a week at Zapdemo Labs

Everything in here is made up. The people, customers, emails and prices are fictional, but the problems are real ones we had this month.

**The setup.** Zapdemo is a 4-person company, Austin and Singapore, 13 hours apart. It's Saturday 24 Oct 2026, 3pm in Austin. Here's everything that happened this week. A lot of it has been dropped.

**Your job.** Give each person their own AI. Feed it this week. It should work out what's owed, to whom and by when, put the right person on each piece, and finish what it can. When we restart it fresh, the next one carries on without asking.

## What's in the box

| Folder | What | Real tool it stands in for |
|---|---|---|
| `team.json` | The 4 people, roles, time zones, what each owns | your org chart |
| `calls/` | 10 call notes | Granola |
| `inbox/` | 15 emails, sent and received | Email |
| `slack/` | Messages from the team channels | Slack |
| `linear/` | The task backlog (some tasks have no owner) | Linear |
| `calendar/` | Upcoming events | Calendar |
| `whatsapp/` | Messages from customers | WhatsApp |

Load it into real tools (free Slack, Linear, a test inbox) or read the files directly. Both are fine.

## Drops

At each check-in (4:30, 6:00, 7:30) and at lights out (8:30), we post new emails and messages in the Slack channel. Feed them into your setup. Some change earlier facts, and your AI has to notice.

## Rules for the AI

- Nothing goes to a real person or a real address. Every address ends in `.example`.
- Payments are test mode only.
- If unsure, it asks the owner, not the room.
- It never makes up a price, a date or a fact. If it isn't in the pack, it doesn't exist.
